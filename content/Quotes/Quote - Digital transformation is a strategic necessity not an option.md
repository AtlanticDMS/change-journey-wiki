## The quote

> Embracing digital transformation is no longer an option; it is a strategic necessity to survive amidst changing market dynamics, digital monetary exchange systems, rising competition and shifting customer expectations.

— *[[AI and Digital Transformation - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]*, Introduction

## Why I saved this
The clearest statement of urgency in the book. Pairs directly with Vol 1's urgency-building step in the [[Kotter 8-Step Model]] — the "why change" argument applies as much to digital transformation as to any organisational change.
