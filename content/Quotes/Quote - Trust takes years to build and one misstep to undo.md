## The quote

> Trust is however quite fragile, taking years to build and only one misstep to undo, so engagements should always be sincere and transparent.

— *[[Change Management - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]*, Ch. 3

## Why I saved this
Directly relevant to change leadership in culturally sensitive contexts. A reminder that the human infrastructure of trust is always the most fragile part of any transformation.
