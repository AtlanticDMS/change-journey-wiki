## The quote

> Business and community leaders should recognize that change is not a discrete event, but a continuous process woven into the fabric of modern socio-economic dynamics.

— *[[Change Management - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]*, Introduction

## Why I saved this
Challenges the common framing of change as a project with a start and end date. Has implications for how organisations should be structured — not for episodic change, but for continuous adaptation.
