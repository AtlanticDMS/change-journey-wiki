## The quote

> Stay Curious. Stay Bold. Stay Transformed.

— *[[AI and Digital Transformation - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]*, Epilogue

## Why I saved this
The closing call to action of the book — and of the series so far. Curiosity maps to continuous learning; boldness to strategic risk-taking; "transformed" to the recognition that transformation is an ongoing state, not a destination. Pairs with Vol 1's quote that change is a continuous process.
