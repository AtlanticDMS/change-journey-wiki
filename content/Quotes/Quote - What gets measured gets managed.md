## The quote

> What gets measured gets managed.

— [[Peter Drucker]], cited in *[[Data Insights and Analytics - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]*, Ch. 7

## Why I saved this
The most concise articulation of why measurement matters. Bridges all three volumes — Vol 1's [[KPIs for Change]], Vol 2's digital performance metrics, and Vol 3's marketing ROI measurement all rest on this principle. What you choose to measure shapes what you choose to manage.
