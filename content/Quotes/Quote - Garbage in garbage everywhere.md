## The quote

> In the world of marketing analytics, the adage "garbage in, garbage out" rings particularly true. Clean, reliable, accurate data is the foundation of actionable insights and effective decision-making. Once garbage data is in your system, it permeates everything downstream. If your data is not squeaky clean from the start, it's garbage in garbage everywhere. It is as polluting as microplastics.

— *[[Data Insights and Analytics - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]*, Ch. 2

## Why I saved this
The most practically important warning in the book. No amount of sophisticated analytics saves you from poor data quality. Connects directly to Vol 2's data management principles and makes the investment in [[Data Quality and Integrity]] practices non-negotiable.
