## The quote

> Organisations do not change unless the people do.

— *[[Change Management - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]*, Ch. 1

## Why I saved this
The single most important sentence in the book. It reframes change management entirely — away from systems and processes, toward human behaviour and psychology. Everything else in the book flows from this premise.
