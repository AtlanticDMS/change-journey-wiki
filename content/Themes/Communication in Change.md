## What is this theme?
This refers to any communication used during any of the phases of a change process. The communication is specifically designed to inform stakeholders about any aspect of the change process. This is a broad definition and can relate to any context related to a change process.

The principle that how change is communicated — the narrative, the timing, the channel, the tone — is as important as the change itself. Communication is not just information transfer; it is meaning-making.

## Why this theme matters
Poor communication is the most cited cause of change resistance. Well-designed communication transforms sceptics into champions.

## How different books approach it

### [[Change Management - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
Dedicates a full chapter to communication strategy and uses the PunkVision parable to show how CEO Sarah Punkett's storytelling approach — blending data with narrative, using multiple channels, and maintaining two-way feedback — turned employees into change advocates.

### [[Data Insights and Analytics - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
Data Storytelling (Ch. 9) is a direct contribution to this theme — the ability to translate data into narrative is the same skill as communicating change. Both require knowing your audience, leading with meaning not mechanics, and combining evidence with human context.
