## What is this theme?
The recurring principle across both volumes that planning, assessment, and alignment must precede implementation — whether the implementation is a change initiative (Vol 1) or a technology deployment (Vol 2).

## Why this theme matters
The most common and costly failure mode in both change management and digital transformation is premature action — moving to implementation before the organisation is ready, aligned, and clear on objectives.

## How different books approach it

### [[Change Management - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
Change Readiness assessment, Leadership Alignment, and Change Coalition formation all precede any implementation activity. Kotter's first three steps — urgency, coalition, vision — are entirely pre-implementation.

### [[AI and Digital Transformation - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
Chapter 2 (Digital Readiness Assessment) and Chapter 3 (Digital Transformation Strategy) must both be completed before any technology is deployed. The ByteBright parable starts with assessment, not with tool selection.

## Key concepts under this theme
- [[Change Readiness]] — Vol 1 pre-implementation assessment
- [[Digital Readiness Assessment]] — Vol 2 pre-implementation assessment
- [[Leadership Alignment]] — Vol 1; alignment before action
- [[Digital Transformation Strategy]] — Vol 2; strategy before deployment
- [[Kotter 8-Step Model]] — Vol 1; steps 1–3 are all pre-implementation

## My synthesis
Both volumes converge on the same principle from different directions. This is the most important cross-volume insight in the series: readiness and strategy are not overhead — they are the investment that makes everything else work.
