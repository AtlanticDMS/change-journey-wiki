### [[Change Management - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
Vol 1 frames change as continuous, not episodic: "Change is not a discrete event but a continuous process." Ongoing Support Mechanisms sustain improvement post-transition.

### [[AI and Digital Transformation - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
Chapter 12 is entirely dedicated to post-transformation maintenance, continuous improvement, and innovation. Agile and Lean Practices provide the operating methodology. Knowledge Management captures learnings for organisational reuse.

### [[Data Insights and Analytics - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
Vol 3 Ch. 13 frames data analytics itself as a continuous improvement discipline — analytics strategy must be regularly revisited, tools updated, and methods refined as the business grows. Marketing ROI measurement creates the feedback loop that drives campaign optimisation. The book's five-act conclusion frames the analytics journey as ongoing, not a destination.
