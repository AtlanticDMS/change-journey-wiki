## Definition
An organisation's demonstrated capacity — in terms of leadership commitment, employee skills, resources, and culture — to absorb and successfully execute a change initiative before it begins.

## Why it matters
The series treats readiness assessment as the non-negotiable first step of any transformation, planned or digital. Skipping it is identified as one of the most common and costly mistakes SMB leaders make — comparable to "measuring twice, cutting once." Assessing readiness surfaces gaps, risks, and resistance points while they are still cheap to address.

## How it works
- **Technology infrastructure audit** — evaluating existing systems, hardware, and compatibility with planned change
- **Data readiness** — assessing quality, availability, and governance of internal and external data
- **Talent and skills audit** — identifying capability gaps and openness to change
- **Leadership commitment check** — gauging whether leadership is genuinely aligned before asking the rest of the organisation to follow
- **Stakeholder analysis** — mapping who will be affected and anticipating resistance early

## Examples from the books
- From [[AI and Digital Transformation - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]] (Ch. 2): a seven-step Digital Readiness Assessment framework covers scope definition, technology inventory, skills assessment, data management evaluation, business risk assessment, customer feedback analysis, and roadmap creation.
- A fictional bakery discovers via readiness assessment that its outdated point-of-sale system — not its product — is driving negative social media sentiment, prompting a targeted (not wholesale) digital upgrade.
