## What is this theme?
The principle that effective leaders ground their decisions in evidence — data, research, measurement, and honest assessment — rather than in hierarchy, intuition alone, or the pressure to appear decisive. Evidence-based leadership does not eliminate judgment; it ensures judgment operates on accurate information.

## Why this theme matters
The most common failure mode across all three volumes is leaders acting on bad information — whether that is a poorly assessed change readiness (Vol 1), an underestimated digital risk (Vol 2), or a gut-based marketing decision built on poor data (Vol 3). Evidence-based leadership is the meta-competency that makes every other framework work.

## How different books approach it

### [[Change Management - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
Implicitly frames evidence-based leadership through the KPI system — leaders who don't measure can't manage. The overconfidence bias warning in Communication Strategy is a direct call to epistemic humility. Leadership Alignment requires honest assessment of where leaders actually are, not where they think they are.

### [[AI and Digital Transformation - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
The Digital Readiness Assessment is fundamentally an evidence-gathering exercise before action. The strategy prioritisation matrix (impact vs feasibility) requires honest, data-backed assessment of both dimensions. Risk management requires evidence of what risks actually exist.

### [[Data Insights and Analytics - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
The entire book is an argument for evidence-based leadership. DYOR, data-driven decision making, and the anti-bias framework in Ch. 7 all build toward the same competency. Peter Drucker's "what gets measured gets managed" and Deming's "all others must bring data" are both citations of this theme.

## My synthesis
This is the deepest cross-volume theme in the series. Every volume argues, from a different angle, that leaders who act on evidence outperform those who act on assumption. The series as a whole is a toolkit for building evidence-based leadership capability across the human, technology, and data dimensions of business.
