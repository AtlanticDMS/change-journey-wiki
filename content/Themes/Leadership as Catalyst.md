## What is this theme?
The idea that change does not happen without leaders who actively model, champion, and embody the transformation — not just endorse it strategically. Leaders are not just architects of change; they are its most visible signal.

## Why this theme matters
Employees watch what leaders *do*, not just what they say. Misaligned or disengaged leaders are the fastest way to kill a change initiative's credibility.

## How different books approach it

### [[Change Management - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
Runs this theme throughout every chapter. Distinguishes between organisational leadership (sets vision) and dedicated change leadership (executes tactics). Both must be aligned and behaviorally committed. The BubbleTech parable illustrates how CEO Emily Bubblebuzz's one-on-one work with resistant leader Mark Bubbleman was decisive.

### [[AI and Digital Transformation - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
The epilogue's fourth key takeaway is "Leadership Commitment" — explicitly stating that leadership provides vision, support, and resources that empower teams through transformation. Chapter 4 notes that leaders must model digital behaviours, not just endorse them — a direct echo of Vol 1's leadership alignment concept.
