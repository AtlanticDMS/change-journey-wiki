## What is this theme?
The idea that technology adoption decisions are not IT decisions — they are strategic business decisions. The question is never "what technology should we buy?" but "what business outcome are we trying to achieve, and what technology best enables it?"

## Why this theme matters
SMBs that treat technology as a cost centre make defensive, reactive decisions. SMBs that treat technology as a strategic asset make offensive, opportunity-driven decisions. The latter consistently outperform.

## How different books approach it

### [[AI and Digital Transformation - A Guidebook for SMB Leaders]]
The entire book argues this thesis. Chapter 3 on strategy explicitly frames technology prioritisation through an impact vs feasibility lens. The epilogue's five key takeaways all frame technology as a strategic lever, not an operational tool.
