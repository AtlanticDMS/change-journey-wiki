## Definition
A class of AI with the ability to create new content — text, images, code, audio, or video — based on simple natural language instructions (prompts). Powered by Large Language Models (LLMs). Examples include ChatGPT (OpenAI), Copilot (Microsoft), Gemini (Google), and Claude (Anthropic).

## Why it matters
Generative AI has democratised content creation and code generation at unprecedented scale and speed. For SMBs, it compresses tasks that previously required specialists — copywriting, report drafting, customer communication, basic coding — into minutes with minimal cost.

## How it works
LLMs are trained on vast amounts of text and other data. They learn statistical patterns in language and can generate coherent, contextually appropriate responses to prompts. "Prompt engineering" — the skill of crafting effective instructions — is now a recognised professional discipline.

The book discloses that Generative AI was used selectively in the creation of the Change Journey series itself — a practical demonstration of the technology's business utility.

## SMB use cases
- Marketing copy and content generation
- Customer email drafting and personalisation
- Report summarisation and synthesis
- Basic code generation and debugging
- Customer service chatbot responses
- Competitor research and analysis
