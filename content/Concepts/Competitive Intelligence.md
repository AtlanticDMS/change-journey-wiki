## Definition
The systematic gathering, analysis, and application of information about competitors, market dynamics, and industry trends to inform strategic positioning and decision-making.

## Why it matters
SMBs that understand only their own performance operate in a vacuum. Competitive intelligence situates your performance within the market context — revealing whether growth is genuinely strong or simply keeping pace with a rising tide.

## How it works
**Key competitive intelligence activities:**
- **Competitive benchmarking** — comparing your metrics (conversion rates, customer retention, pricing, product features) against industry standards and direct competitors
- **Market trend analysis** — identifying emerging trends before they become mainstream; spotting disruption signals early
- **Competitor positioning analysis** — understanding how competitors communicate, price, and position their offerings
- **Win/loss analysis** — systematically understanding why deals are won or lost relative to competitors

**SMB-accessible tools:**
- SEMrush, SimilarWeb — competitor web traffic and SEO analysis
- Social listening tools — monitoring competitor brand mentions and sentiment
- Google Alerts — tracking competitor news and PR
- Industry reports (Gartner, Forrester) — market trend benchmarking
