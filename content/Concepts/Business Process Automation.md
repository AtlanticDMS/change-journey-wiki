## Definition
The use of digital technologies — ranging from simple workflow tools to Robotic Process Automation — to streamline repetitive, manual business tasks, reducing errors, cutting costs, and freeing employees to focus on higher-value work.

## Why it matters
For SMBs with limited headcount, automation is one of the highest-leverage digital transformation investments available. It converts hours spent on manual data entry, invoice processing, or inventory tracking into time available for strategic, creative, or customer-facing activity — while reducing the error rate inherent in manual work.

## How it works
1. **Process mapping** — document existing workflows end-to-end to identify inefficiencies and automation candidates
2. **Employee engagement** — involve the people who perform the tasks daily, since they hold the clearest view of pain points
3. **Software evaluation** — identify requirements, shortlist tools, run trials/demos, assess integration with existing systems
4. **Phased rollout** — pilot on a limited scale (e.g. one product line or department) before expanding
5. **Continuous monitoring** — track KPIs (cycle time, error rate, cost savings) and refine post-implementation
