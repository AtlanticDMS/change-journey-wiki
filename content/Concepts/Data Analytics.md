## Definition
The systematic collection, processing, and interpretation of data to generate actionable business insights. Ranges from descriptive analytics (what happened) through diagnostic (why it happened), predictive (what will happen), and prescriptive (what should we do).

## Why it matters
Data analytics converts raw operational data into competitive intelligence. For SMBs, even basic analytics capability dramatically improves decision quality — replacing intuition-based choices with evidence-based ones.

## How it works
**Data collection** — identify relevant data sources: CRM systems, website analytics, transaction records, customer feedback, social media, operational systems. Prioritise data quality over quantity.

**Analysis** — use tools appropriate to scale:
- Small teams: Google Analytics, Power BI, Tableau, Excel
- Growing teams: Salesforce analytics, HubSpot reporting, Zoho Analytics
- Advanced: custom ML models, data warehouses (AWS Redshift, Google BigQuery)

**Actioning insights** — analytics value is only realised when insights drive decisions. Establish clear processes for translating dashboard findings into operational changes.

## Types of customer data (from Ch. 7)
Demographic, geographic, psychographic, behavioural, transactional, engagement, social media, feedback, support interaction, browsing, referral, purchase frequency, satisfaction, email engagement, churn, CLV, lead source, loyalty program, and syndicated survey data.
