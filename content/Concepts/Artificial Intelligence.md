## Definition
The simulation of human intelligence by computer systems — encompassing the ability to learn from data, reason toward conclusions, solve problems, understand language, and perceive visual input. An umbrella term covering many distinct subtypes.

## Why it matters
AI is the central enabling technology of the current wave of digital transformation. For SMBs, AI tools that were previously enterprise-only are now accessible at low cost, fundamentally changing the competitive landscape.

## Key subtypes and their business relevance

- **[[Machine Learning]]** — systems that improve from experience without reprogramming; powers recommendation engines, demand forecasting, fraud detection
- **[[Generative AI]]** — creates new content (text, images, code) from prompts; powers tools like ChatGPT, Copilot, and content automation
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** — enables computers to understand and generate human language; powers chatbots, voice assistants, sentiment analysis
- **Computer Vision** — enables computers to interpret visual data; powers facial recognition, quality control, medical imaging
- **Large Language Models (LLMs)** — AI trained on vast text datasets; foundation of most modern generative AI tools
- **[[Robotic Process Automation]]** — software bots that automate rule-based tasks; bridges pre-AI legacy processes with modern automation

## Real-world SMB applications
- Customer service chatbots (NLP)
- Personalised product recommendations (ML)
- Demand and sales forecasting (ML)
- Content and marketing copy generation (Generative AI)
- Invoice and document processing (Computer Vision / OCR)
- Virtual assistants for scheduling and admin (NLP + LLMs)
- Fraud detection (ML)

## Related concepts

- [[Robotic Process Automation]] — automation that bridges AI and legacy process
