## Definition
A dedicated, cross-functional team assembled to lead and drive a change initiative. The coalition should include diverse skills and representation from different levels of the organisation, with enough influence to drive change across departments.

## Why it matters
Change that is pushed from the top alone rarely sticks. A coalition creates multiple points of ownership and influence, accelerating adoption and surfacing resistance earlier.

## How it works
Key skills to include in the coalition:
- **Communication skills** — articulate the vision clearly at all levels
- **Influence and persuasion** — gain buy-in from reluctant stakeholders
- **Technical expertise** — credibility in the domain being changed
- **Emotional intelligence** — navigate resistance, anxiety, and interpersonal dynamics
- **Adaptability** — respond to real-time challenges during implementation

The coalition should have access to affordable tools for project management, collaboration, messaging, and training — AI-enabled tools are increasingly viable at low cost.
