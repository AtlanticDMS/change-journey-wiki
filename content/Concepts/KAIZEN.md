## What is this theme?
Embracing methodologies such as [[LEAN+]] Six Signa, [[Agile+]] practices, or [[KAIZEN]] fosters a [[Culture+]] of iterative refinement, where every process, product, or service is subject to scrutiny and enhancement. Combining this with a digital-first philosophy, and you are on your way to becoming future proof.

[[Continuous Improvement]] initiatives, fuelled by advancements in management philosophies like [[LEAN+]] or Six Sigma, foster a [[Culture+]] of innovation and efficiency.

Incremental change, often characterized by its gradual and iterative nature, involves making small, cumulative adjustments to existing processes, systems, or structures. This approach is akin to a series of continuous improvements or fine-tuning initiatives, where organizations seek to enhance efficiency, optimize operations, and address specific challenges.

[[Continuous Improvement]] is essential to ensure that training efforts align with the dynamic nature of the transformation.
