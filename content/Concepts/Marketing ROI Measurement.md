## Definition
The systematic measurement of the financial return generated by marketing activities relative to the cost of those activities. Encompasses attribution modelling, budget optimisation, channel performance analysis, and emerging measurement technologies.

## Why it matters
Without ROI measurement, marketing budget allocation is guesswork. SMBs with limited budgets cannot afford to waste spend on underperforming channels or attribution models that misattribute success.

## How it works
**Attribution models — who gets the credit?**
- **Last-click** — credits the final touchpoint before conversion; simple but misleading (ignores earlier influence)
- **First-click** — credits the first touchpoint; overvalues awareness, undervalues conversion
- **Multi-touch / time-decay** — distributes credit across all touchpoints, weighted toward recent interactions; more accurate; the ecoChic Threads case study showed a 20% CPA reduction after switching to this model
- **AI-driven attribution** — continuously refines itself as new data arrives; Google's Enhanced Attribution is a current example

**Budget optimisation techniques:**
- Focus on high-performing channels (regular conversion rate and CPA analysis)
- Dynamic budget reallocation — shift spend toward channels with better returns
- Embrace marketing automation to reduce cost-per-engagement
- Prioritise customer retention — acquiring a new customer costs significantly more than retaining an existing one
- A/B testing — continuously test messaging, creative, and offers

**Emerging trends:**
- AI-driven attribution models (automated, adaptive)
- Blockchain for advertising transparency (fraud prevention, verified impressions)
- Real-time ROI dashboards (live performance data enabling instant pivots)
