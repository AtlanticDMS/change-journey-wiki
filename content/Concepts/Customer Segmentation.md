## Definition
The process of dividing a customer base into distinct groups (segments) based on shared characteristics — demographic, geographic, psychographic, behavioural — and tailoring marketing, communications, and product offerings to each segment's specific needs and preferences.

## Why it matters
Generic marketing wastes budget and alienates customers. Well-executed segmentation drives higher engagement rates, conversion, and long-term loyalty by making customers feel seen and understood.

## How it works
1. Define segmentation criteria relevant to your business (see [[Data Analytics]] for data types)
2. Build segments using CRM and analytics tools (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Facebook Ads Manager)
3. Craft personalised messaging and offers for each segment
4. Automate delivery using email and CRM automation tools (Mailchimp, Marketo, ActiveCampaign)
5. Refresh segments periodically — customer behaviour evolves

**Common mistakes to avoid:**
- Overly broad segments that miss nuance
- Neglecting to update segments over time
- Over-segmentation — too many segments become unmanageable
- Violating data privacy regulations
