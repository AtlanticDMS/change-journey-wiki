## Definition
The delivery of computing services — servers, storage, databases, networking, software, and analytics — over the internet on a pay-as-you-go basis. Eliminates or radically reduces the need for on-premise physical infrastructure.

## Why it matters
Cloud computing is the infrastructure layer that makes most other digital transformation initiatives possible. It gives SMBs access to enterprise-grade computing power without the capital expenditure, while enabling the scalability and remote access that modern businesses require.

## Three service models

**IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service)**
Virtualised computing resources — servers, storage, networking — rented on demand. Best for businesses needing infrastructure flexibility. Providers: AWS (EC2, S3), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Also consider decentralised alternatives like Filecoin or Arweave for data sovereignty concerns.

**PaaS (Platform as a Service)**
Development environment provided as a service — tools, libraries, and infrastructure for building and deploying applications. Relevant for businesses developing software or custom applications. Providers: Heroku, Google App Engine, Azure App Service.

**SaaS (Software as a Service)**
Software delivered via browser on subscription — no installation or maintenance required. The most accessible model for SMBs. Providers: Salesforce (CRM), Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, HubSpot.

## Migration approach (ByteBright parable)
Start with non-critical workloads — email, video conferencing, file storage. Build confidence and operational knowledge. Then migrate progressively to CRM and mission-critical systems. Phased migration reduces risk and builds team capability.
