## Definition
The integration of digital technology into all aspects of a business, fundamentally changing how it operates and delivers value to customers. For SMBs, it is a strategic shift — not just a tool upgrade — that enhances competitiveness, improves efficiency, and drives growth.

## Why it matters
Digital transformation has moved from competitive advantage to survival requirement. SMBs that fail to adapt face displacement by digitally native competitors, changing customer expectations, and macroeconomic pressures they are not equipped to respond to.

## How it works
Digital transformation spans a spectrum of initiatives:

- **Process digitisation** — converting manual or paper-based processes to digital equivalents
- **Technology integration** — adopting AI, cloud, CRM, and automation tools
- **Cultural transformation** — building a digital-first mindset across the organisation (see [[Digital Culture]])
- **Customer experience redesign** — reimagining how customers interact with the business across digital channels
- **Data-driven decision-making** — using analytics to inform strategy rather than intuition alone

Key historical drivers: cost efficiency, productivity gains, globalisation, and accelerating technological advancement. Key future drivers: AI/ML, IoT, cloud computing, and cybersecurity demands.

## Relationship to change management
Digital transformation is the technology strategy. [[Change Management]] (Vol 1) is the human strategy. Neither succeeds without the other.

- [[Leadership Alignment]] (Vol 1) must precede any transformation initiative
- [[Skill Gap Analysis]] (Vol 1) identifies human capability gaps the technology will expose
