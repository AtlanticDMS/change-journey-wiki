## What is this theme?

Internal improvements may be driven by a commitment to excellence and a pursuit of operational efficiency, and may also contribute to the inevitability of change within organizations. [[Continuous Improvement]] initiatives, fuelled by advancements in management philosophies like [[LEAN]] or Six Sigma, foster a culture of innovation and efficiency.
