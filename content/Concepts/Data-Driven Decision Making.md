## Definition
The practice of grounding business strategies, marketing decisions, and operational choices in quantifiable, evidence-based insights rather than intuition, hierarchy, or convention. Data-driven organisations treat data as a primary input into every significant decision.

## Why it matters
A Harvard Business Review study cited in the book found that data-driven companies are 5% more productive and 6% more profitable than those that don't use data-driven strategies. For SMBs with limited resources, data-driven decision making also minimises the cost of mistakes — validating assumptions before committing resources.

## How it works
**Three core benefits:**
1. **Higher ROI** — data identifies which strategies and channels yield the best returns before budget is committed
2. **Minimised risk** — testing assumptions (A/B testing, pilot studies) avoids costly misallocations
3. **Optimised resource allocation** — limited SMB budgets are directed to proven-effective activities

**Common SMB barriers:**
- Limited in-house expertise → solution: outsource data work or use self-service tools
- Cultural resistance → solution: build [[Data-Driven Culture]]; demonstrate quick wins
- Tool cost/complexity → solution: start with free tools (Google Analytics, HubSpot free tier, Tableau Public)
