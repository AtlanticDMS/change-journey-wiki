## Definition
The active inclusion of employees in decision-making processes during change initiatives, cultivating a sense of ownership, reducing resistance, and leveraging the expertise of those closest to the work.

## Why it matters
Employees who have a voice in shaping change are far more likely to embrace it. Involvement transforms people from passive recipients of top-down directives into active co-authors of transformation.

## How it works
- Invite participation in key decisions relevant to employees' roles
- Use open forums, town halls, anonymous feedback tools, and one-on-one conversations
- Treat employee input as a genuine resource — not a compliance exercise
- Build two-way communication into every phase of the change, not just the announcement stage
- Acknowledge and visibly incorporate feedback so employees see their input has impact
