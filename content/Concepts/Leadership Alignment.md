## Definition
The state in which all organisational leaders share a unified commitment to and understanding of the change vision. A precondition for successful change; inconsistencies in leadership messaging cause confusion and resistance throughout the organisation.

## Why it matters
Leadership misalignment is one of the most common and damaging failure points in change initiatives. Employees take their cues from leaders — if leaders send conflicting signals, the entire change effort loses credibility and momentum.

## How it works
- Convene leadership meetings (virtual and in-person) to align on vision before communicating it broadly
- Use data and AI-generated future scenarios to make the case compelling to sceptical leaders
- Create space for individual leaders to raise concerns privately before public commitment is expected
- Establish ongoing check-ins, progress updates, and shared success stories throughout the change
- Leaders must actively model desired behaviours — not just endorse them verbally
