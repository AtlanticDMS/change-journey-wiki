## Definition
Systematic patterns of deviation from rational judgment that distort how researchers and analysts collect, interpret, and act on data. Awareness of these biases is essential for producing reliable, actionable insights.

## Why it matters
Even with excellent data and sophisticated tools, human interpretation introduces bias at every stage. Unexamined biases lead to decisions that feel data-driven but are actually rationalisations of pre-existing beliefs.

## Key biases in research (from Ch. 7)

| Bias | Description | Counteraction |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmation bias** | Seeking data that confirms existing beliefs | Pre-register hypotheses; actively seek disconfirming evidence |
| **Hindsight bias** | "I knew it all along" after the fact | Record predictions before outcomes are known |
| **Overconfidence bias** | Overestimating prediction accuracy | Calibration exercises; peer review |
| **Selection bias** | Non-random sample selection | Random or stratified sampling |
| **Survivorship bias** | Focusing only on successes | Include failures and non-survivors in analysis |
| **Recency bias** | Overweighting recent events | Analyse over longer periods; use historical benchmarks |
| **Framing bias** | Different presentation → different interpretation | Test multiple framings of the same data |
| **Social desirability bias** | Respondents give socially acceptable answers | Anonymise responses; use indirect questioning |
| **Groupthink** | Conformity overrides critical thinking | Encourage dissent; use Delphi method; involve external reviewers |
| **Cognitive dissonance** | Dismissing data that contradicts beliefs | Create psychological safety for data that challenges assumptions |

## General counteraction strategies
- Regular training on cognitive biases for all who interpret data
- Triangulation — use multiple data sources to cross-validate
- Peer review by those not involved in the research
- Diverse research teams to challenge homogeneous thinking
- Transparent documentation of assumptions and data choices
- Pilot studies before full-scale research
- Statistical controls for known biases
