## Definition
The shared values, beliefs, and behaviours that guide how people within an organisation think and act — described in the series as the "soul" of a business, complementing structure (its "skeleton").

## Why it matters
The series treats structure and culture as inseparable: without strong structures, even the best culture dissolves into chaos; without healthy culture, the most efficient structures become empty routines. Preserving core cultural values during transformation — rather than letting technology or restructuring erode them — is repeatedly framed as a condition for long-term organisational survival.

## How it works
- **Structure vs culture** — structure is the tangible framework (processes, hierarchies, systems); culture is the intangible meaning and purpose that animates it
- **Organisational homeostasis** — lasting businesses achieve a stable state where structure and culture reinforce each other
- **Cultural pillars** — core values should be explicitly defined, communicated, and integrated into recruitment, performance management, and daily decision-making
- **Vulnerability during transformation** — major change makes existing cultural values more fragile and in greater need of deliberate reinforcement
