## Definition
A structured evaluation of an organisation's current technological capabilities, processes, workforce skills, and cultural readiness before embarking on digital transformation. The equivalent of a diagnostic before prescribing treatment.

## Why it matters
Transformation without readiness assessment is one of the most common causes of failed initiatives. It leads to underestimating resource requirements, encountering unexpected skills gaps, and misaligning technology choices with actual business needs.

## How it works
The book outlines a 7-step readiness assessment process:

1. **Technology audit** — inventory current systems, tools, and infrastructure; identify gaps and redundancies
2. **Process mapping** — document existing workflows to identify inefficiencies and automation opportunities
3. **Skills assessment** — evaluate digital literacy and technical capabilities across the workforce
4. **Customer experience audit** — assess current digital touchpoints and pain points in the customer journey
5. **Data audit** — evaluate data collection, storage, quality, and usage practices
6. **Financial assessment** — model the investment required vs expected ROI across transformation initiatives
7. **Competitive benchmarking** — compare digital maturity against industry peers to identify urgency and opportunity
