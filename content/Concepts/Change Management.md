## Definition
A structured, systematic approach to transitioning individuals, teams, and organisations from a current state to a desired future state. It encompasses planning, implementation, and reinforcement of change initiatives.

## Why it matters
Organisations that fail to manage change actively risk becoming obsolete. Without structure around change, transitions create confusion, resistance, talent loss, and wasted resources.

## How it works
Three core phases:
1. **Planning** — analyse the need for change, identify drivers (internal or external), define the desired future state
2. **Implementation** — execute plans, communicate clearly, ensure stakeholders understand their roles
3. **Reinforcement** — embed changes into culture, celebrate wins, provide ongoing support, align leadership behaviour
