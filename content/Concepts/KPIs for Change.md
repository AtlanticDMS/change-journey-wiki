## Definition
Key Performance Indicators used to measure the progress and effectiveness of a change initiative. Provides the change leadership team with an ongoing gauge of momentum and a basis for proactive intervention.

## Why it matters
Without measurement, change management is guesswork. KPIs enable early detection of resistance, skill gaps, and communication failures — and justify course corrections before they become crises.

## How it works
The book identifies 10 core KPIs for change initiatives:

1. **Employee Engagement Index** — survey scores on commitment, motivation, satisfaction
2. **Adoption Rate** — % of employees effectively using new processes or technologies
3. **Change Readiness Score** — composite readiness assessment via surveys and interviews
4. **Communication Effectiveness** — feedback scores from change-related communication channels
5. **Employee Productivity Metrics** — output per employee, project completion rates
6. **Training Completion Rates** — % of employees who completed required training
7. **Employee Turnover Rates** — % leaving during or shortly after the change period
8. **Resistance Levels** — reported resistance instances from surveys or direct channels
9. **Time-to-Adoption** — average time for employees to demonstrate proficiency in new behaviours
10. **Customer Satisfaction Metrics** — customer scores related to the impact of the change

NLP-enabled AI tools can automate the collection and analysis of many of these metrics, especially sentiment-based ones like engagement and communication effectiveness.
