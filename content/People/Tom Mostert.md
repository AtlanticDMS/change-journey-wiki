## Who are they?

Tom Mostert spent more than 30 years inside the data analytics and marketing industry, helping global brands use behavioral science and audience targeting to capture consumer attention at scale.

He helped build the systems that inform media and marketing platforms which emotional states kept people engaged longer.

But as a commercial sociologist, he wasn't merely interested in profitable outcomes. He was monitoring how the mechanisms he was using to boost consumer engagement was operating in his own life, and the lives of others. What he observed was not just disturbing, it exposed the hidden architecture of psychological exploitation and deliberate manipulation that we call the attention economy.

Extreme Fear is the result of years of critical observation of this system. A rigorously researched field guide to how the attention economy manufactures chronic anxiety across six domains of daily life, and what ordinary people can concretely do to get out of the red. It draws on clinical frameworks including trigger stacking and allostatic load, spanning the fields of behavioral psychology, public health, and economics, and three decades of professional experience inside the industry it critiques.

He writes about the systems rigging your emotions: the business models behind burnout, the design decisions behind the constant dread, and the evidence behind the recovery. His work is for people who want to understand the machine before they try to fix themselves.

Tom Mostert is a commercial sociologist with experience across multiple industries, including healthcare, consumer products, finance, media and technology. He is also the author of the Change Journeys series of business guidebooks for leaders navigating AI-driven digital transformation.

## More Information
CO-FOUNDER OF ATLANTIC DMS

Tom graduated from the University of Johannesburg with a bachelor’s degree in marketing communications and a master’s degree in sociology before building a career as a data insights & analytics expert. He is a seasoned business strategist and compassionate people leader.

Over 30 years in the data insights industry, Tom worked at international agencies and consultancies in Europe and Africa, as well as at global organizations in strategic positions.

A passionate believer in knowledge sharing and people development, Tom is developing a series of tools and resources aimed at helping business leaders to navigate the constantly changing and often disruptive business landscape more successfully.

The CHANGE JOURNEYS Series of business guidebooks is his way of passing on his knowledge and experience to the growing community of entrepreneurs in the Small and Medium Business (SMB) sector.

Principal behind Atlantic DMS, a South African-based digital publishing and business consulting practice with multi-industry, multi-cultural client experience spanning European, African, American, and Asian markets. Credited as author across all three volumes.

## Why it matters
The series' credibility rests heavily on Mostert's practitioner background — direct experience leading culturally diverse teams (cited as spanning Dutch, German, African, American, Asian, Eastern European, and Middle Eastern professionals) and hands-on consulting roles at organisations including Royal Philips and Bayer Pharmaceuticals. This grounds the frameworks in lived practice rather than pure theory, and is the reason the series consistently favours actionable tools over academic abstraction.

## How it works
- Positions the series' philosophy as "unequivocally people-first" — preserving culture while implementing effective change
- Draws on personal family entrepreneurial background (construction, hospitality, game farming) for many of the series' real-world-style examples
- Transparently acknowledges selective use of generative AI in creating, editing, and improving the books' content — treated as a demonstration of the tools the series itself recommends
- Positions the trilogy as a spiral, not a checklist — designed to be revisited as an organisation matures through successive transformation cycles
