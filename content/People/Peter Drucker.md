## Who are they?
Peter Drucker (1909–2005) was an Austrian-American management consultant, educator, and author — widely regarded as the father of modern management theory. His work spanning six decades shaped how organisations think about strategy, knowledge work, and performance measurement.

## Key ideas associated with them
- "What gets measured gets managed" — the principle that only measured performance can be systematically improved
- Management by objectives (MBO)
- The concept of the knowledge worker
- Decentralisation and empowerment as management principles
