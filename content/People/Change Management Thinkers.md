## Who are they?
John Kotter is a Harvard Business School professor and one of the world's foremost authorities on leadership and organisational change. Best known for developing the 8-Step Change Model and for the Harvard Business Review article "Leading Change: Why Transformation Efforts Fail" (1995).

## Key ideas associated with them
- [[Kotter 8-Step Model]] — sequential framework for managing planned organisational change
- The concept of creating urgency as the critical first step in any change initiative
- Distinction between management (handling complexity) and leadership (handling change)

## Books they wrote
- *Leading Change* (1996)
- *Our Iceberg Is Melting* (2006)

## Who are they?
Kurt Lewin (1890–1947) was a German-American social psychologist, widely regarded as the founder of modern social psychology. His work on group dynamics, action research, and field theory laid the groundwork for organisational change management as a discipline.

## Key ideas associated with them
- [[Lewin Change Model]] — the foundational Unfreeze / Change / Refreeze framework
- Field Theory — behaviour is a function of the person and their environment
- Force Field Analysis — identifying driving and restraining forces in any change situation

## Books they wrote
- *Field Theory in Social Science* (1951)

## Who are they?
Jeff Hiatt is the founder of Prosci, a leading change management research and training organisation, and the creator of the ADKAR model. His work focuses on the individual human dimension of organisational change.

## Key ideas associated with them
- [[ADKAR Model]] — individual-focused change model: Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement
- The idea that organisational change only succeeds when each individual successfully makes their own personal transition

## Books they wrote
- *ADKAR: A Model for Change in Business, Government and our Community* (2006)
