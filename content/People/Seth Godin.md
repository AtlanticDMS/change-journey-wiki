## Who are they?
Seth Godin is an American author and marketing theorist known for writing influential books on modern marketing, permission marketing, and the idea economy. Author of Purple Cow, Permission Marketing, and Tribes, among others.

## Key ideas associated with them
- "Don't find customers for your products, find products for your customers" — customer-centric product development
- Permission marketing — earning attention vs interrupting for it
- Tribes — the power of leading communities of people with shared interests
- The idea that being remarkable (Purple Cow) is the only viable marketing strategy
