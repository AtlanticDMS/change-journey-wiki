## Who are they?
Founder of Prosci and creator of the ADKAR Model, a goal-oriented, individual-level framework for change adoption. Author of *ADKAR: A Model for Change in Business, Government and Our Community* (2006).

## Why it matters
Hiatt shifted the centre of gravity in change management theory from the organisation to the individual. Where Kotter and Lewin describe what leadership and the organisation must do, Hiatt's ADKAR model describes what must happen *inside each person* for change to stick — making it a uniquely diagnostic tool for identifying exactly where an adoption effort is breaking down.

## How it works
- Founded Prosci, now one of the most widely licensed change management research and certification bodies globally
- Developed ADKAR from large-scale research into what differentiates individuals who successfully adopt change from those who do not
- Positioned ADKAR as a "bottom-up" framework — building organisational success in business and community transformations from the accumulation of successful individual transitions
