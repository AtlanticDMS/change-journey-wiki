## Who are they?
Claude Shannon (1916–2001) was an American mathematician and electrical engineer, widely credited as the "father of digital circuit design theory" and the founder of information theory. His 1948 paper "A Mathematical Theory of Communication" laid the mathematical foundation for all modern digital communication, computing, and data compression.

## Key ideas associated with them
- Information theory — quantifying information as "bits" and establishing the theoretical limits of data transmission
- Digital circuit design — demonstrating how Boolean algebra could be applied to electrical circuits, enabling the first digital computers
- The conceptual foundation for all modern computing, internet communication, and AI
