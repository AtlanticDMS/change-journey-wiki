## The central thesis of the series
Sustainable organisational transformation requires both human change management and technology strategy — executed simultaneously, not sequentially. Vol 1 without Vol 2 produces aligned people with no tools. Vol 2 without Vol 1 produces powerful tools with nobody willing to use them.
