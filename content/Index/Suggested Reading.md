> A curated list of authoritative external books mapped to the central themes of the Change Journey series. Use this to deepen any concept or theme note with primary-source material beyond what Atlantic DMS references directly.

## Change management & leadership
- **John P. Kotter** — *Leading Change* (1996) and *Leading Change: Why Transformation Efforts Fail* (HBR, 1995) — the primary source behind [[Kotter 8-Step Model]]
- **Kurt Lewin** — *Field Theory in Social Science* (1951) — the primary source behind [[Lewin Change Model]]
- **Jeff Hiatt** — *ADKAR: A Model for Change in Business, Government and Our Community* (2006) — the primary source behind [[ADKAR Model]]
- **Edgar H. Schein & Peter A. Schein** — *Organizational Culture and Leadership* (5th ed., 2017) — deepens [[Culture]] and [[Digital Culture]]
- **Kim S. Cameron & Robert E. Quinn** — *Diagnosing and Changing Organizational Culture* (3rd ed., 2011) — practical companion to [[Cultural Sensitivity in Organisations]]

## Cross-cultural and organisational intelligence
- **P. Christopher Earley & Soon Ang** — *Cultural Intelligence: Individual Interactions Across Cultures* (2003) — the academic foundation of [[Cultural Intelligence]]
- **Geert Hofstede, Gert Jan Hofstede & Michael Minkov** — *Cultures and Organizations: Software of the Mind* (3rd ed., 2010) — deepens [[Cultural Sensitivity in Organisations]] with national cultural dimension theory

## Lean, Agile, and continuous improvement
- **Eric Ries** — *The Lean Startup* (2011) — see [[The Lean Startup]]; deepens [[Agile]] and [[Continuous Improvement]]
- **James P. Womack & Daniel T. Jones** — *Lean Thinking: Banish Waste and Create Wealth in Your Corporation* (1996) — the primary source behind [[LEAN]]
- **Thomas J. Peters & Robert H. Waterman** — *In Search of Excellence* (1982) — foundational to the McKinsey 7-S model referenced alongside [[Agile]] and organisational adaptability

## Disruption, innovation, and digital strategy
- **Clayton M. Christensen** — *The Innovator's Dilemma* (1997) — see [[The Innovator's Dilemma]]; deepens [[Digital Transformation]] and [[Risk Management in Digital Transformation]]
- **Thomas M. Siebel** — *Digital Transformation: Survive and Thrive in an Era of Mass Extinction* — direct companion to [[Digital Transformation Strategy]] and [[Technology as Business Strategy]]

## Data, analytics, and evidence-based decision-making
- **W. Edwards Deming** — foundational quality-management thinking behind "In God we trust, all others must bring data," cited directly in [[Evidence-Based Leadership (Theme)]]
- **Peter Drucker** — foundational management thinking behind "What gets measured gets managed," cited directly in [[Evidence-Based Leadership]]
- **Katie Delahaye Paine** — *Measure What Matters: Online Tools for Understanding Customers, Social Media, Engagement, and Key Relationships* — deepens [[Marketing ROI Measurement]] and [[Data Storytelling]]

## Data ethics and privacy
- Recommended follow-up research: primary GDPR and CCPA regulatory texts, and post-Cambridge Analytica case analyses, to deepen [[Ethical Data Use]] beyond the series' summary treatment
