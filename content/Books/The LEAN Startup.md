## Definition
A 2011 business methodology book by Eric Ries, listed in the Recommended Reading section of the Change Journey series. Introduces the "build-measure-learn" feedback loop and the concept of validated learning as an alternative to traditional, plan-heavy product development.

## Why it matters
Although not written specifically for SMBs, its core ideas — iterative development, rapid experimentation, and treating failure as a source of learning rather than a setback — align directly with the Agile and Lean philosophies that Vol II of the Change Journey series recommends for sustaining innovation after digital transformation.

## How it works
- Build-Measure-Learn loop: create a minimum viable product, measure real customer response, learn and iterate
- Validated learning: treat every product decision as a hypothesis to be tested with real data, not assumption
- Pivot or persevere: use evidence to decide whether to continue a strategy or change direction
