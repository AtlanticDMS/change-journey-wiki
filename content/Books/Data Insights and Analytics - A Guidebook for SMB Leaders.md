## Summary
A comprehensive, practical guidebook for SMB leaders on marketing intelligence, data analytics, and evidence-based decision-making. Covers the full journey from understanding why data-driven decisions matter, through building an analytics framework and data-driven culture, to advanced techniques like predictive analytics, customer analytics, competitive benchmarking, ROI measurement, data storytelling, and ethical data use. The most technically detailed volume in the series and the operational backbone of Vol 2's data strategy concepts.

## Core argument
Evidence-based decision-making is no longer a luxury for SMBs — it is a survival necessity. Data-driven organisations consistently outperform competitors, and the democratisation of analytics tools means SMBs can now access capabilities once reserved for large enterprises. The key is not collecting more data, but extracting better insights from the right data.
