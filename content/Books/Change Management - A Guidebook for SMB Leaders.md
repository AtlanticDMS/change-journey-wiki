## Summary
A practical guidebook for small and medium business leaders navigating organisational change. Focuses exclusively on the human side of transformation — covering change models, cultural intelligence, communication strategy, employee engagement, and leadership alignment. Explicitly excludes digital transformation economics, and headcount reduction as out-of-scope topics covered in other volumes.

## Core argument
Successful organisational change is fundamentally a human challenge, not a structural or technological one. Organisations do not change unless the people do.

## Key concepts
- [[Change Management]] — structured approach to transition individuals, teams, and organisations to a desired future state
- [[Incremental vs Radical Change+]] — spectrum from gradual adjustments to paradigm-shifting transformation
- [[Planned vs Unplanned Change+]] — deliberate initiatives vs reactive responses to unforeseen events
- [[Kotter 8-Step Model]] — widely used sequential framework for planned change
- [[Lewin Change Model]] — three-stage Unfreeze / Change / Refreeze framework
- [[ADKAR Model]] — individual-focused model: Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement
- [[Cultural Intelligence]] — understanding and navigating cultural differences in change contexts
- [[Leadership Alignment]] — ensuring leaders are unified behind the change vision
- [[Change Coalition]] — diverse, influential team assembled to drive transformation
- [[Change Readiness+]] — assessing organisational preparedness before implementing change
- [[KPIs for Change]] — metrics for measuring change initiative progress
- [[Skill Gap Analysis]] — 12-step framework to identify and address capability needs
- [[Communication Strategy]] — crafting narratives and selecting channels for change messaging
- [[Employee Involvement+]] — participative decision-making to build ownership and reduce resistance
- [[Ongoing Support Mechanisms+]] — feedback, mentorship, training, and emotional support post-change
